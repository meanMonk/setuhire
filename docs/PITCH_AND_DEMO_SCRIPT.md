# SetuHire — Pitch Positioning & Demo Video Script

## Positioning (X helps Y achieve Z)

**SetuHire gives IT staffing/bench-sales teams one system for candidates, vendors, clients, and margin — so recruiters stop digging through email and the owner stops flying blind on who to call and what's pending.**

Short version for decks/emails:
> "SetuHire helps IT staffing agencies turn scattered email threads into a searchable candidate/vendor database with AI matching and margin visibility."

## Who it's for
- **Primary buyer:** owner/CEO of a small-to-mid IT staffing or bench-sales shop (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Full-stack, AI/ML placements) — the person currently reconciling margin in Excel and asking recruiters "who's working what."
- **Daily users:** recruiters/account managers juggling multiple vendors and clients, currently living in Outlook/Gmail search.
- **Signal that someone's a fit:** they mention managing both a direct candidate pool *and* vendor/supplier relationships, bill on TSC vs sourcing cost margin, and still track pipeline in spreadsheets or email.

## The pain, in their language (from PRD §1)
1. Candidate and vendor-supply info buried in email — no central search.
2. No idea which vendor has the right supply for a tech stack until someone remembers or searches old threads.
3. New client job openings go cold — no follow-up tracking.
4. Owner has no bird's-eye view of who's working what, which candidates are hot, who to call next.
5. JD-to-candidate matching is tribal knowledge in one person's head.
6. FTE vs Contract not distinguished, but margin/billing logic differs.
7. No margin visibility (TSC vs sourcing cost vs commission) without manual Excel reconciliation.
8. No clean view of invoiced vs paid vs pending across clients and vendors.

## What it does (map pains → features, 4 max for the pitch)
1. **Centralized DB** — clients, vendors, candidates, jobs in one searchable place (kills #1, #2).
2. **Dashboard + "Needs Your Attention" feed** — auto-surfaces who to follow up with today (kills #3, #4).
3. **AI JD-to-candidate matching** — paste/URL a JD, get ranked candidates with a match score and a plain-English rationale; ≥90% flagged as priority-call (kills #5).
4. **Margin + Finance view** — TSC vs sourcing cost vs commission per placement, billing status at a glance, CSV export for the accountant (kills #6, #7, #8).

## How you work (don't put prices)
Custom-built around their existing process — not a rigid SaaS template. They own the data. Scoped after a short call.

## Demo video script (walkthrough, ~5–7 min)

Screen recording + voiceover, one take, seed/dummy data (not real client data).

**0:00–0:15 — Hook**
"If you run an IT staffing or bench-sales desk, you probably have candidates in one inbox, vendor supply lists in another, and margin numbers in an Excel sheet nobody trusts. I built SetuHire to fix that. Quick walkthrough."

**0:15–0:45 — Dashboard**
"This is the owner's view the moment you log in — open jobs, available candidates, pending billing, all in one place." Point at the **Needs Your Attention** feed: "This is the part that actually saves time — it tells you '3 candidates matched 90%+ on this job and nobody's called them yet,' or 'this vendor hasn't responded in 5 days.' That's the follow-up list you used to keep in your head."

**0:45–1:30 — Clients page**
Expand a client row inline: "Each client expands to show their open jobs, right there — no clicking into five tabs." Click into detail: "Margin per job — Target Selling Cost vs sourcing cost vs your commission — shown per engagement, not one blended number."

**1:30–2:15 — Vendors page**
"Same idea for vendors — expand to see which candidates they've supplied and where those candidates stand." Point at performance stats: "Submission-to-placement ratio per vendor — so you know who's actually delivering vs who's just noise."

**2:15–3:30 — Jobs + AI Match (the core demo moment)**
"Here's the part that replaces tribal knowledge." Paste a JD URL or raw text: "It parses the JD automatically — must-have skills, experience, employment type." Click **Run AI Match**: "Now it ranks every candidate in your DB against this job. Anything 90%+ gets flagged — that's your call-today list. And each one comes with a rationale, not just a score." Read one rationale line aloud.

**3:30–4:00 — Candidates page**
"Upload a resume, it parses it into a structured profile — skills, experience, education — so it's searchable and matchable going forward instead of sitting as a PDF in someone's inbox."

**4:00–4:45 — Finance page**
"And this is the number the owner actually cares about at month-end — revenue, commission earned, pending receivables, pending vendor payouts. Export to CSV, hand it to your accountant."

**4:45–5:15 — Close**
"That's SetuHire — one system for candidates, vendors, clients, and margin, built around how an IT staffing desk actually works, not a generic CRM. If this looks like your mess, reply and tell me what's most painful for you right now."
